73. Final cheat sheet for your viva
If guide asks...	Say...
What is classification report?	Class-wise evaluation of the model
Precision?	Of predictions made as a class, how many were correct
Recall?	Of actual samples of a class, how many were found
F1?	Harmonic mean of precision and recall
Support?	Number of actual test samples in that class
Confusion matrix?	Table showing actual classes versus predicted classes
Rows?	Actual classes
Columns?	Predicted classes
Diagonal?	Correct predictions
Off-diagonal?	Misclassifications
What is median?	Middle value after sorting observations
Why median?	More robust to extreme values
Where does Normal Median come from?	Calculated from Normal-class sensor values in the dataset
Where does Fault Median come from?	Calculated from the corresponding fault-class sensor values
Is median an engineering threshold?	No, it is a data-derived statistical reference
Does dataset change affect median?	Yes
Does dataset change affect model results?	Yes, potentially all results
What is percentage change?	Relative difference between fault median and normal median
What are contributing parameters?	Sensors showing the largest data-derived differences from Normal
Do they prove causation?	No
What are discovered patterns?	Behavioural groups found using clustering of learned LSTM representations
Are they physical fault subtypes?	No, they are exploratory behavioural patterns
What is silhouette?	A measure of how separated the clusters are
Why use Normal as baseline?	To describe how fault-labelled data differs from normal operation
What is fault occurrence?	A contiguous run of the same predicted fault label
Is it a physical failure event?	No, not without physical event IDs
Why 85.79% accuracy?	1262 of 1471 test sequences were correctly classified
Why macro F1 only 74.78%?	Performance varies substantially across classes, especially Faults 1–3
The single most important thing to remember

Your project has two separate ideas:

The LSTM performs classification.

and

The statistical/clustering analysis explains the observed behaviour in the data.

The second part does not magically prove physical causes. That distinction will make your explanation much more technically correct.

If your guide asks about any exact number in these files, the safest approach is to trace it back to the underlying dataset calculation or model predictions, rather than saying it was a predefined value.
